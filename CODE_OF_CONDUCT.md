# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to keep participation in **Crawler Labyrinth** harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming for everyone regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus reviews and discussions on technical correctness, security, accessibility, and maintainability.
- Respect differing technical viewpoints and give actionable, polite feedback.
- Assume good intent and prioritize clear reproduction steps when reporting issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or trolling.
- Publishing private information of other contributors without permission.
- Submitting intentionally malicious payloads, undocumented telemetry, or obfuscated code.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported via the maintainer contact page at [arnoldwender.com](https://arnoldwender.com).
